# Welcome to the Fabric

Welcome.

This space is built from simple things: words, weight, movement, and a little bit of imagination.

There is no wall between you and the page.

**Enjoy the existence in the fabric of spacetime.**

Everything here has weight.

Every element you encounter presses gently against the fabric beneath it. Every click, every scroll, every movement shifts something somewhere. The page bends around what you touch, curves beneath what you move, and settles again when you leave it alone.

Nothing is perfectly still.

The smallest interaction can send a ripple outward.

Sometimes, every twenty seconds or so, the fabric remembers that it is alive — a gravitational wave passing through the space, bending everything in its path before fading back into quiet.

So explore.

Click something.

Scroll slowly.

Move things around.

Watch how the space responds.

The page is not merely displaying its contents. **It is reacting to your presence.**

Perhaps that is the point.

A space does not exist independently of what inhabits it. Things have weight because they are there. Paths bend because something passed through them. Waves travel because something disturbed the stillness.

This blog is my small experiment with that idea.

Built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the `hugo-noir` theme, with a few layout overrides and a lot of room left for whatever comes next.

For now, there are only words.

But even words have weight.

And this is the first thing I've placed on the fabric.

Welcome.

Stay for a moment.

See what moves.
